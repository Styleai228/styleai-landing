Cookie Policy

Effective: May 25, 2026 Last updated: May 25, 2026 Version: 1.0

This policy explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on our website (trystyleai.app) and inside the Style.AI mobile application. We aim for the minimum that keeps things working — and nothing that tracks you across the internet.

01What cookies are

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. They help the site remember preferences, recognize you across pages, and measure how the site is used. Mobile apps don't use cookies in the strict sense — they use similar local identifiers and SDK storage. This policy covers both.

02Cookies we use

CategoryPurposeLifetimeRequired?
Strictly necessary Maintain your session, security tokens, language preference Session — 12 months Yes
Performance & analytics Understand which pages and features people use, detect errors Up to 14 months No — opt-in
Affiliate attribution Identify when a purchase came from a Style.AI link, so the retailer credits us Up to 90 days (set by retailer) No — only set when you click an outbound link
What we DO NOT use No advertising cookies. No cross-site behavioral tracking. No social network pixels. No fingerprinting techniques designed to bypass cookie controls.

03Third-party cookies and SDKs

When you click a retailer link, the retailer (and its affiliate network — Amazon, Rakuten, Awin, Impact, etc.) may set a cookie on your device. That cookie is governed by the retailer's policy, not ours. In the mobile app, we use SDKs from providers such as Supabase and Sentry — these may set local identifiers for crash reporting and aggregated usage metrics. You can disable analytics in the app's privacy settings.

04How to manage cookies

On our website:

  • The cookie banner (if shown in your region) lets you accept or reject non-essential cookies.
  • You can clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.
  • Most browsers offer a "Do Not Track" or privacy mode.

In the mobile app:

  • Toggle analytics in Settings → Privacy.
  • iOS users can revoke tracking permission via iOS Settings → Privacy → Tracking.

Disabling strictly necessary cookies may break the site or app. Disabling analytics is safe and changes nothing for you as a user.

05Changes to this policy

We update this policy when our cookie usage changes. The "Last updated" date at the top reflects the current version. Material changes will be announced through our site or app.
