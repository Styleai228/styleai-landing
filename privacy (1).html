03How we use your data

Your data is used strictly to:

  • Generate personalized outfit recommendations from photos you submit.
  • Save your preferences and history so you can return to past looks.
  • Display product results from retail partners that match your profile.
  • Track affiliate link interactions to receive commission attribution and measure recommendation quality.
  • Fix bugs, improve the app, and prevent abuse.
  • Send you transactional and (with consent) marketing communications.
  • Comply with legal obligations applicable to the operator.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share it with advertisers. We do not use your photos to train third-party AI models.

04Legal basis for processing

Where the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the UK GDPR, or similar laws apply, we rely on:

  • Consent — when you upload a photo for AI analysis. You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting the data in-app.
  • Contract — to provide the styling service you signed up for.
  • Legitimate interests — to keep the service secure, prevent fraud, analyze usage, attribute affiliate clicks, and improve AI quality.
  • Legal obligation — when we must retain data for tax or legal compliance.

06AI processing and your rights

Style.AI uses artificial intelligence — specifically image analysis, computer vision, natural language processing, and content generation — to analyze your photos and generate personalized styling recommendations. Our AI is provided by OpenAI (GPT-4o vision model).

You can opt out of AI processing at any time by:

  • Not uploading photos — AI processing only activates when you voluntarily submit a photo.
  • Deleting previously uploaded photos from your in-app history.
  • Disabling camera and photo library permissions in iOS Settings.
  • Updating your account settings in the app.
  • Contacting privacy@trystyleai.app to request manual deletion.

Our AI does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects on you — recommendations are informational and inspirational only.

07Data retention

We keep your data only as long as needed:

  • Photos: processed in real time. Stored in your history only if you opt in, and deletable anytime in-app.
  • Profile data: kept while your account is active. Deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Crash logs and aggregated usage metrics: retained up to 12 months in anonymized form.
  • Legal records (transactions, tax): retained for the period required by Kyrgyz law, typically up to 5 years.

08Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the data we hold about you.
  • Correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Delete your account and associated data.
  • Object to processing based on legitimate interests.
  • Export a copy of your data in a machine-readable format.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.
  • Unsubscribe from marketing communications via the link in any email or in-app notification settings.
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority in your country.

To exercise any right, email privacy@trystyleai.app. We respond within 30 days.

09Security

We use TLS encryption for data in transit and encryption at rest for stored data. Access to production systems is limited and logged. We follow industry-standard security practices including authentication tokens, row-level security on databases, and secret management. No system is 100% secure, but we apply strong safeguards. If a breach occurs that affects your data, we will notify you and the relevant authorities as required by law, including within 72 hours under GDPR.

10Children's privacy

Style.AI is not directed at children under 13 (or under 16 in EU/EEA). We do not knowingly collect data from such children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact privacy@trystyleai.app and we will delete it.
