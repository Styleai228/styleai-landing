1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files that websites place on your device when you visit them. They allow the website to remember your actions and preferences (such as language, font size, and other display preferences) over a period of time, so you don't have to re-enter them whenever you return to the site or browse from one page to another.

Mobile applications do not technically use cookies in the same way websites do. Instead, they use local identifiers, SDK storage, device identifiers, and similar technologies. For simplicity, this Cookie Policy covers both web cookies and these mobile equivalents.

6. How to Control or Disable Cookies

6.1 In Your Browser

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can:

  • View what cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete cookies individually or all at once
  • Block cookies from specific websites
  • Block all third-party cookies
  • Set your browser to notify you when a cookie is being placed

For browser-specific instructions, visit:

  • Google Chrome
  • Safari (macOS)
  • Safari (iOS)
  • Mozilla Firefox
  • Microsoft Edge

Please note: blocking strictly necessary cookies may impair the functionality of the Site, including login and security features.

6.2 In Our Mobile Application

You can control mobile tracking and identifiers through your device settings:

  • App Tracking Transparency: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → toggle apps off
  • Advertising identifier: Settings → Privacy & Security → Apple Advertising → toggle off Personalized Ads
  • Push Notifications: Settings → Notifications → Style.AI → toggle off
  • Analytics opt-out: In-app Settings → Privacy → Analytics → toggle off

6.3 Global Privacy Control (GPC) and Do Not Track

Our Site honors the Global Privacy Control (GPC) signal where applicable under U.S. state privacy laws (such as CCPA/CPRA). When we detect a GPC signal in your browser, we will treat it as a valid opt-out request for the sale or sharing of personal information.

We currently do not respond to legacy "Do Not Track" (DNT) browser headers, as no consistent industry or legal standard for them exists. However, we do not engage in cross-site behavioral tracking regardless of DNT status.
