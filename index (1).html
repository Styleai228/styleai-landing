FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my photo stored?

Photos are processed to generate recommendations and stored only with your consent for history features. You can delete them anytime from the app.

Where do the clothes come from?

We integrate with established retail catalogs through affiliate networks. We never hold inventory — you buy directly from the original seller.

Do you take a cut of my purchase?

We earn a small affiliate commission from retailers when you buy through our links. This is paid by the retailer, not you. The price is the same as on their site.

Which countries are supported?

Style.AI launches globally on the App Store. Retailer availability varies by region — we'll show only options that ship to your location.

Is there a free version?

Yes. Core analysis and recommendations are free. Style.AI Pro unlocks unlimited looks, saved wardrobes, and priority styling.
